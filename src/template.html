Details

About this model

A reconstruction of Sembawang Brook (HDB BTO 2026) from the brochure site plan and photos of HDB's 1:300 sales model. It is not a survey, and the brochure says layouts and facilities are subject to change.

What is measured, read or estimated

  • Read: storeys (30; 26 for 438A; 8 for the car park), the flat-mix table, unit numbers, labels, road names.
  • Measured: block positions and footprints from the site plan (scale 0.708 m/px, checked three times, within 0.2%), the 25.5° estate grid, the canal, roads and linkways. Facade patterns and roof features come from the model photos.
  • Derived: storey 1 is a void deck, because every block's flat count divides exactly by its residential floors (290 = 10 × 29 …).
  • Estimated: 2.8 m floor-to-floor and a 3.6 m void deck (consistent with the model photos; editable under Heights), plus fixed estimates for the car-park height (~24 m) and the low-rise heights.
  • Representative: trees, play equipment, shelters and roof-garden items are placed from the photos to look right. They are not surveyed.

Known uncertainties

  • Unit labels #116 and #118 (Blk 437A) follow the numbering pattern of the other blocks; the brochure digits are illegible. Flat types are certain.
  • Which Blk 438A tower holds the 1-Room vs the 2-Room rental flats isn't stated.
  • Neighbouring reserved sites are shown flat; buildings planned there could change views and sun.
  • The brochure notes the estate is close to a heavy vehicle park (possible noise); its location is pinned, its extent is not shown on the plan.
  • True north is taken from the brochure's north arrow. The latitude/longitude used for the sun is approximate (Sembawang).

Controls

  • Orbit drag · Pan right-drag / two-finger drag · Zoom wheel / pinch
  • Select tap a block (tapping a facade also picks the stack under your finger), a pin, a road or a reserved site
  • Views buttons at the bottom, or keys 1–7; R reset; F focus selection; compass = face north
  • Search /: "437B", "#120", "#12-160", "4-room", "playground", "Admiralty"
  • Measure M: tap two points; Esc to finish
  • Walk: drag to look, WASD/arrows to move, Shift faster; on phones use the left pad
  • Sun: pick a date and time (Singapore time), or play the day
